# G02 — Encrypted NAS backups

**Status:** The unreviewed G02 candidate is healthy on the Pi. Actual encrypted NAS delivery and repository integrity have passed. Actual isolated restore, human review/merge and merged-source reconciliation remain pending; G03 is gated.

G02 captures consistent scoped PostgreSQL state, selected native SQLite databases, conversation and mission history, artifacts, credentials and recovery journals directly into the existing encrypted vault. The independent uploader streams these copies into a dedicated encrypted Restic v2 repository and retires local copies only after authenticated remote readback. NAS waits do not hold the main service's checkpoint operation lock.

The dedicated account is non-admin. Its private share permits that account and NAS administrators, denies guests and the other users, and disables syncing and its recycle bin. All ten pre-existing shares deny the account. The original private folder identity and mode `0700` were revalidated. Existing NAS users, shares and unrelated backups were preserved. The actual Pi connection and repository admission require signed SMB 2.1.
