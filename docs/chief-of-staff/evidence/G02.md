Status: implementation in progress. G02 acceptance, deployment and human review are pending.

The reviewed G01 release has passed its actual Pi acceptance checks. G02 adds bounded encrypted queues, consistent database capture and archive streaming on the Mac. No G02 release has been deployed. The dedicated NAS account, private share and folder have been created and inspected. The account is non-admin and denied all ten pre-existing shares; the new share allows the backup account and NAS administrators, denies other users and guests, and has syncing and its recycle bin disabled. The folder is private with mode `0700`. The encrypted repository remains pending.
