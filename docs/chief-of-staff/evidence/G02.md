**Status:** The corrected G02 candidate passed all 19 release gates and is healthy on the Pi. Its installed reader has authenticated and validated the complete actual predeployment archive from the NAS. Selected test settings are staged in the encrypted vault. Actual SQL restore acceptance remains pending approval to provision an empty separate test database: the existing test scope contains 30 rows and cannot be overwritten. G02 captures consistent scoped PostgreSQL state, selected native SQLite databases, conversation and mission history, artifacts, credentials and recovery journals directly into the existing encrypted vault. The independent uploader streams these copies into a dedicated encrypted Restic v2 repository and retires local copies only after authenticated remote readback. NAS waits do not hold the main service's checkpoint operation lock.

The dedicated account is non-admin. Its private share permits that account and NAS administrators, denies guests and the other users, and disables syncing and its recycle bin. All ten pre-existing shares deny the account. The original private folder identity and mode `0700` were revalidated. Existing NAS users, shares and unrelated backups were preserved. The actual Pi connection and repository admission require signed SMB 2.1.
