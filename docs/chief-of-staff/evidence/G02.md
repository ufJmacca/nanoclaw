The dedicated NAS account, private share and folder have been created and inspected. The account is non-admin and denied all ten pre-existing shares; the new share allows the backup account and NAS administrators, denies other users and guests, and has syncing and its recycle bin disabled. The folder is private with mode `0700`. The encrypted repository remains pending.
