- The existing user service is active. Its installation, SQLite/session/group paths and service permissions were inspected without changing its invocation or restarting it. The active checkout is clean at `77bf7dea28d35df7bbd7a86ddf899535b38065f5`.

- Runtime and migration credentials were placed in separate private Pi-owned files. They have **not** been injected into the live service. Their paths and target identifiers remain in private records.
- One private Mattermost channel was identified with exactly the bot and one human member. Its dedicated subscription is active and uses Codex. This is connectivity evidence; no CoS binding or live CoS action has been activated.
